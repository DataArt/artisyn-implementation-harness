**Reviewed:** 2026-10-06

**Scope note:** this review covers the `harness-bootstrap` skill at `.canonical-implementation-harness/` in the `artisyn-harness` repo — its entrypoint `SKILL.md`, `harness-bootstrap.md`, `scripts/`, `README.md`, `registry.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `references/`, and `patterns/`. ## Verdict: ✅ PASS

_Triage score: 10/100 (informational only — the verdict above is the gate)_

No CRITICAL, HIGH, or MEDIUM findings. Two LOW findings: the capability scope is declared in prose rather than a structured `permissions:` block, and one local bookkeeping directory (`state/objects/`) has no stated retention policy for its non-secret baseline-byte cache. Neither holds the gate; both are housekeeping.

## Findings summary

0 CRITICAL · 0 HIGH · 0 MEDIUM · 2 LOW · 12 PASS · 5 N/A

## What has to change

Nothing blocking. Housekeeping (LOW):

1. Add a structured `permissions:` declaration (or equivalent machine-readable capability block) alongside the existing prose `Capabilities` field, so least privilege is reviewable without parsing free text.
2. Add a one-line retention/cleanup statement for `state/objects/<sha256>` (e.g. "pruned when no longer referenced by an unresolved baseline/merge") even though its content is non-secret.
