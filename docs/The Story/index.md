# The Story

The why behind DataArt's platform: the industry-wide story, the common challenges the harness exists to solve, and the mindset shift adopting it requires.

**ADLC** is the structured, evidence-led discovery process this story comes from: diagnosing an organisation's actual SDLC bottlenecks — not assumed ones — and mapping a concrete path to AI-first workflow adoption from there, through a Strategic Intent → Vision → Problem Space → Solution Goals → Solution → Roadmap arc.

Content belongs here if it answers "why does the platform exist and what is it trying to solve?"
